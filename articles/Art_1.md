# On The Iranian Situation

On February 28th the Trump administration launched a barrage of rockets on Iran. According to the president of the United States, the operation aims at defending the American people, American bases in the Middle East, protecting its allies and preventing Iranian nuclear program.
Though there is no denying the troubling actions of the Iranian regime, and though I personally wish to see the mollahs’ influence end, the recurring violation of international law can't be ignored. We aren't new to this. In the past, Europe was quick to follow U.S. policy without independent consideration. As Europe matures the realization that it could very well be the next target, and in some ways has already been, it needs to counter the US and push its own agenda forward.

I am not a prophet, nor am I claiming to reveal anything new. Our leaders know it. Yet Europe remains complicit and dependent on America.

French president, Emmanuel Macron, tweeted


>The Iranian regime must understand that it now has no other option but to engage in good faith in negotiations to end its nuclear and ballistic programs, as well as its regional destabilization activities

It is difficult to see Iran's responsibility on this specific incident, given they were hit first. The nuclear threat narrative remains deeply contested. Khamenei was strongly against the acquisition, development and use of nuclear weapons so much so he baked it into a fatwa. Let's remember that Iran in 2015 agreed to extensive monitoring and restriction of activities at nuclear facilities, from which Donald Trump unilaterally withdrew in 2018. Meanwhile Israel developed nuclear capabilities covertly and continues to deny them.

Merz's tweet echoes Macron’s philosophy, emphasizing the Iranian people's right to decide their own fate. Yet this appears more pretext than principle. I do not believe either the U.S. or Europe acts in the interest of the Iranian people. Nonetheless, European leadership seems largely aligned on this rhetorical line, though it is difficult to believe they truly embrace it.


What does this attack really bring us? What if the regime collapses and Iran plunges into chaos? Who pays the consequence? Not the U.S., not Israel but Europe. Is Europe ready for another wave of migrants coming from the Middle East? Are we ready for a more destabilized Middle East that will impact us much more than the US? As Zelensky put it, they have a nice ocean and won't feel it as we will. We are all tired of Europe playing second fiddle to the U.S., of not being a credible partner, despite the enormous potential. Why don't we build our own strategy? Let's become a real international actor and not mere bystanders. For that to happen we need a more compact and united Europe. Until we won't achieve it, we will be forced to turn the blind eye and to play America's game.
