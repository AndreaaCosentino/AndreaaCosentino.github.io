# A tragic tradition of irrelevance: Italy has got war sorted

It has been very disheartening to read about the state of support to Ukraine in Italy. There are the usual supects. As always. The obvious ones who are against, such as leader of the League Salvini who has been long suspected to have strong ties to Russia. But this time he isn't alone.  On the right Vannacci follows closely, and for good reason. He has worked in Moscow for the Italian Embassy and has a visceral love for Russia. Till up to the day of the invasion of Ukraine he was sure it was a hoax, as reported from people who worked with him at the time. 

Nevertheless, even if proven wrong, during his brief political life he supported "peace" , but peace isn't fair according to him and we should recognise Russia has the upper hand. "Italy first" is his mantra, but as a general he should recognise that victory against Russia, or at least of avoiding a defeat, is very well in the interest of Italy and Italians. It's obvious that he's tapping into public discontent towards the war in Ukraine, but what's bewildering is how angst for armament in Ukraine pervades Italy, from left to right and from bottom to top. 

Amongst the bigger parties, the exception is Meloni. It is an expectional exception. Meloni knows supporting Ukraine is costing her politically, but I think it's something she genuinely believes in. Before coming to power she advocated for more military spending arguing that, no country is really free if it can't defend itself. Yes, the support hasn't been great and surely Italy could have done more, but the point still stands. This isn't playing both sides as we have seen her doing multiple times, she has fully committed to the Ukrainian cause. 

Now the more painful part of it all. One could expect the left to either be compact in support of Ukraine or at least some part of it to be in favour of sending armaments. Or at least, that would be my expectation, which I understand is misplaced trust. Let's start with movement 5 stars: the third political party for number of votes and a left wing populist party. Not only they are against, but it's their flagship policy. Giuseppe Conte, leader and former pm, argues that we are prolonging the war and playing with WW3. He doesn't believe being meek and tame is an invitation for more in the future.

Spearheading the left coalition is Elly Schlein, who to be fair has been in favour. But she has been timid and shy on the topic, and understandably: she doesn't want to support Ukraine. The democrats are in fact split : the moderate ones supporting Ukraine and being pro NATO, and the others, which Elly is part of. Her public support is an effort to keep the party together, not a real political line. One wonders what would happen were she to win. Bersani, which was leader of the democrats and one of the founders, says we should keep supporting Ukraine. But, in his words, people in Europe should shut their mouth, and Von Der Leyen should calm down. Accordint to him, Europe is provoking Russia. 

Let me give you 2 last names, not politicians but important figures in the Italian cultural landscape. First, Alessandro Barbero. This man's status in Italy is huge, one of, if not, the best historians. I adored him for so long.

> If war will come, it will because of us 

> How do we wage war on Russia?

> The new German army will pass through Poland and Ukraine to invade Russia?

> Not only I don't believe Russia will invade Italy, but not even Kallas' home in Estonia. 

*(Translation made by me on the fly)*. And I must conclude with Francesca Albanese, stern supporter of Gaza. She not only believes we should stop sending guns and tanks to Ukraine, but that this very war is an American plot to keep Europe weak, ignoring the fact that it did the very opposite. 

**My opinion**. I find the situation to be disheartening. In Italy there seems to be a big "anti-war" sentiment, which I believe stems from article 11 of the constitution *"Italy rejects war"*. Though, first this is fake pacifism, you are for peace when you support the weakest and the one attacked. The appeasement policy doesn't really work , if anything it encourages the aggressor and makes it more bold. Second, article 11 is never read fully and understood: nowhere does it say to be cowards and Russian's doormats.
