About

I hold a Master's degree in Quantum Information from Sorbonne Université. My thesis, carried out at IRIF (Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale) under the supervision of Arthur Braida, studied how analog quantum computers can be used to tackle the Quantum MaxCut problem.

I am now looking for a PhD position at the intersection of quantum algorithms and theoretical computer science. If my interests fit your group, I'd be glad to hear from you.

Research interests

  • Quantum algorithms
    Designing and analysing algorithms for quantum computers, from analog to gate-based models.
  • Quantum simulation
    Algorithms for simulating quantum many-body systems efficiently.
  • Graph theory
    Combinatorial optimization on graphs and its quantum counterparts, such as Quantum MaxCut.
  • Semialgebraic proofs
    Proof systems and hierarchies, such as sum-of-squares, for certifying bounds on classical and quantum optimization problems.
